Privacy policy

Doable · last updated 27 October 2026

In short

Doable has no servers and no accounts of its own. Your data is stored on your own devices. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data, and Doable contains no ads, analytics or trackers.

Data on your device

Everything you enter (tasks, notes, photos, passwords and so on) is stored only on the device you use. Passwords are additionally encrypted. Deleting the app or its website data deletes this data.

Optional sync with Google

Only if you turn on sync and sign in with Google:

  • Doable asks Google for two permissions: access to a hidden app data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata) and your email address (to show which account is connected). Doable cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Doable stores your data in that folder as files that are encrypted on your device with a sync passphrase only you know (AES-256). Google stores only the encrypted files; the makers of Doable never receive them.
  • Google sign-in data stays between your device and Google. Doable keeps the sign-in token only on your device.
  • You can stop at any time: turn off sync in Doable, and remove Doable's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the synced files, remove Doable's hidden app data in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → Doable → Delete hidden app data).

Doable's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Location, camera and microphone

Used only when you choose to (diary location, photos and scans, voice notes) and stored only with your data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. For questions, use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Doable.
