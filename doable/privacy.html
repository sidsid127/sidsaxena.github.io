Privacy policy

Doable · last updated 28 October 2026

In short

You sign in to Doable with Google. Doable's server keeps only your account details (name, email address, profile photo link, when you joined and were last active, your plan). Your tasks, notes, groceries, diary, trips, wallet and credentials stay on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive — encrypted so that only you can read them. We never sell or share your data.

Who is responsible: Sid Saxena, the maker of Doable. Contact: doable.by.sid@gmail.com.

Your Doable account

  • When you sign in with Google, Doable receives your Google account ID, email address, name and profile photo link. These are stored on Doable's server (hosted by Vercel, with the database at Supabase in the EU, Frankfurt) to run your account: to show who is signed in, keep you signed in on your devices, and (later) to let you share with people you invite.
  • We also store when your account was created, when it was last used, which kind of device you use (web or Android), and whether you chose the free plan with ads or Doable Pro.
  • Legal basis (GDPR): performing our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b)). We keep this data until you delete your account.
  • The owner of Doable can see the list of accounts (name, email, dates, plan) to run the service — never any of your content.

Households and sharing

If you create or join a household, or share something with a person, Doable's server keeps the household's name, who its members are (their Doable account), which modules it shares, and pending invitations (including the email address you invite). What you share — lists, tasks, notes, cards — is end-to-end encrypted on your device with a key only the members' devices have; the server stores and passes on only ciphertext. Your sharing key is kept on your devices and in your own Google Drive app folder. Leaving a household removes its shared items from your devices.

Your content

Everything you enter is stored on the device you use. With device protection on, it is encrypted there too. If you turn on sync, Doable stores it in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, encrypted on your device with your sync passphrase (AES-256) before it leaves the device. Neither Google nor the makers of Doable can read it.

Google permissions

  • Your name, email address and profile photo — to create your Doable account.
  • A hidden app data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata) — only for sync. Doable cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.

Doable's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Ads and Doable Pro

Doable may later show a small ad strip to people on the free plan, or offer Doable Pro (no ads) for a one-time payment. Ads are not personalised with your content — your content is encrypted and never available to us or advertisers. If an ad network is added, this policy will name it before it is switched on.

Deleting your data

  • Profile → Delete account deletes your account on Doable's server, Doable's synced files in your Google Drive, and Doable's data on that device. Doable on your other devices keeps what is stored there until you sign out or delete it.
  • Profile → Sign out removes Doable's data from that device (your synced copy stays in your Drive).
  • You can also remove Doable's access to Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your account data, its correction or deletion, restrict or object to its use, and complain to a data protection authority. Write to doable.by.sid@gmail.com.

Location, camera and microphone

Used only when you choose to (diary location, photos and scans, voice notes) and stored only with your content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
