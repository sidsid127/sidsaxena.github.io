Doable

Your private organiser

Doable keeps your to-dos, notes, groceries, passwords, diary, trips, habits, important dates, checklists and wallet cards in one app. It works offline, and your data stays on your devices.

Open Doable

Sync with Google (optional)

If you turn on sync and sign in with Google, Doable keeps one file in a hidden app folder of your own Google Drive, so your phone, iPhone and Mac stay in step. The file is encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know before it is uploaded, so neither Google nor the makers of Doable can read it. Doable cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Install

  • iPhone / iPad: open this site in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Mac: Safari → File → Add to Dock.
  • Android: the Doable app.
