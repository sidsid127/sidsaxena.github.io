🧭 ShopScout
How it works

Your pocket guide for shoppers

Search a brand like Apple or Superdry, a mall like MyZeil, or a city — get store locations, floor directories, and directions instantly.

What ShopScout does

🏷️

Search any brand

Find every store a brand runs across supported malls and cities, with hours and unit numbers.

🏬

Mall floor directories

Say the mall name — like "Zeil" — and get a floor-by-floor directory of what's where.

📍

Location aware

Share your location to see the nearest malls and stores, sorted by distance.

🗺️

Google Maps built in

Every mall and store links straight to a map and turn-by-turn directions.
